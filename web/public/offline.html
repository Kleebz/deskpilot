Can't reach your desktop

This page loaded from the app itself — nothing answered on the network, so the desktop is unreachable rather than broken.

Almost always Tailscale. Check, in order:

  1. Open Tailscale and confirm it is connected
  2. Android may have stopped it in the background — set it to always-on VPN and mark battery use unrestricted
  3. Another VPN may hold the slot; Android allows only one
  4. Confirm the desktop is awake
